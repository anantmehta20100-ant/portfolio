Overview

ForeBid gives a buying agent a market snapshot before it commits to an offer.

Problem

An autonomous agent can negotiate more responsibly when it understands the visible market rather than reacting to one seller in isolation.

Why autonomous agents need market context

Price alone is insufficient. Agent reputation, observation trust, dispersion, and outliers affect how useful a quoted market price is.

Trust-aware pricing

ForeBid combines deterministic snapshot computation with trust-weighted aggregation.

Seven market signals

Byzantine outlier filtering

Outlier handling limits the influence of observations that diverge sharply from the trusted market context.
